WELCOME TO LPYC

A little closer
to the good life.

Rooted in La Porte. Gathered around Pine Lake.

La Porte Yacht Club is a nonprofit 501(c)(7) social and recreational club where friends connect over the water, enjoy a meal and give back to our community. From time at the docks to a night with friends, there’s room to make memories here.

CLUB FRIENDS. COMMUNITY SPIRIT.

Charitable Contributions

Small acts of kindness. A lasting difference.

Join LPYC in supporting our neighbors through club fundraisers, toy collections and community giving.

Give a little holiday joy

Our Home for the Holidays vendor event and bake sale benefits Center Township Trustee’s Christmas Families. The Christmas Toys for Tots Tailgate invites members to donate a toy for the children.
